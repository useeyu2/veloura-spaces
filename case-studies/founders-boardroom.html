The brief

The leadership team needed a boardroom that looked credible to investors and partners, but also supported frequent internal meetings. The brief required comfort, acoustic control, AV readiness, and a disciplined finish palette.

Veloura Spaces delivered the design direction, millwork coordination, furniture specification, lighting control, and contractor oversight.

Execution

We planned the room around sightlines, meeting flow, presentation use, and material durability. Every visible detail was coordinated with the concealed technical layer so the room stayed clean and functional.

Selected details

  • Integrated presentation wall with concealed AV access.
  • Executive table sizing mapped to seating and circulation.
  • Lighting scenes for presentations, negotiation, and informal use.
