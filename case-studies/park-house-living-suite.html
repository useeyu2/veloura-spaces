Park House Living Suite

A layered living environment designed for entertaining, quiet family routines, and a refined material experience without visual excess.

Scope
Interior suite
Timeline
18 weeks
Role
Interior design
Sector
Private residential

Project facts

Primary service
Bespoke Interior Architecture
Design language
Soft modern, walnut, linen, limestone
Client priority
Comfort, storage, and a mature palette

Outcome

  • Improved circulation between lounge, dining, and media zones.
  • Furniture, lighting, and surface finishes specified as one system.
  • Custom joinery added concealed storage without visual heaviness.

The brief

The client wanted a living suite that could move between private downtime and formal hosting. The space needed warmth, acoustic control, display storage, and furniture that felt tailored to the room.

Veloura Spaces developed the interior architecture, furniture direction, lighting layers, material palette, and styling sequence.

Execution

The plan separated conversation, dining, and media without closing the room. A restrained material palette allowed the architectural details to carry the luxury, while lighting scenes gave the suite different evening moods.

Selected details

  • Integrated media wall with concealed cabling and storage.
  • Warm timber, textured upholstery, and quiet metal accents.
  • Layered lighting with task, ambient, and feature circuits.
