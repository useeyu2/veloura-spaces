Crescent House Renewal

A residential renovation that upgraded the building shell, simplified interior flow, and gave the home a sharper long-term material strategy.

Outcome

  • Procurement strategy reduced late substitutions.
  • Final shell created a clean base for furnishing and styling.

The brief

The home needed a technical and aesthetic refresh without turning into a full rebuild. The client wanted a more useful plan, cleaner details, and a stronger finish standard.

Veloura Spaces coordinated the renovation scope, construction sequencing, material procurement, interior shell standards, and handover checks.

Execution

We prioritized structural, moisture, and surface quality before moving into visible finishes. This reduced rework and created a better base for joinery, lighting, and final interior layers.

Selected details

  • Clean wall planes and upgraded floor transitions.
  • Reworked lighting to improve circulation and evening use.
  • Durable finishes selected for long-term family living.
