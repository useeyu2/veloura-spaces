Atelier Work Lounge

A commercial lounge that blends workplace efficiency with hospitality warmth for client meetings, focused work, and team rituals.

Outcome

  • Defined zones for calls, informal meetings, and client hosting.
  • Durable materials specified for high daily use.
  • Lighting and acoustic decisions improved comfort across the space.

The brief

The client needed a workplace area that could support practical operations while presenting a premium face to visiting partners. The space needed to feel calm, not corporate.

Veloura Spaces handled the spatial plan, finish specification, furniture selection, lighting, contractor coordination, and final styling.

Execution

The design separated high-energy team functions from quieter work settings with furniture placement, acoustic treatments, and layered lighting. Material selections were chosen for resilience without losing hospitality character.

Selected details

  • Reception-style lounge seating with commercial-grade upholstery.
  • Acoustic panels integrated into the visual language of the room.
  • Warm lighting scenes for day work and evening hosting.
