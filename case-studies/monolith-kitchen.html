Bespoke joinery

Monolith Kitchen

A sculptural kitchen centered on a stone island, hidden function, and cabinetry that keeps the room calm under heavy daily use.

Scope
Kitchen and joinery
Timeline
10 weeks
Role
Design and procurement
Sector
Private residential

Project facts

Primary service
Bespoke Interior Architecture
Design language
Stone, shadow gaps, integrated appliances
Client priority
Visual calm with professional-grade function

Outcome

  • Improved preparation, storage, and hosting zones.
  • Stone, cabinetry, and lighting coordinated before fabrication.
  • Appliance wall concealed functional density behind clean planes.

The brief

The client wanted a kitchen that looked architectural, stayed easy to maintain, and could support both daily family routines and hosting. The island needed to be the visual anchor without overpowering the room.

Veloura Spaces refined the layout, stone specification, cabinetry drawings, appliance integration, lighting plan, and installation sequence.

Execution

The design paired a strong central island with quieter wall cabinetry. Task lighting was planned into the joinery so the room could remain minimal while still performing under real use.

Selected details

  • Bookmatched stone island with waterfall edges.
  • Handleless cabinetry with integrated storage zones.
  • Warm concealed lighting for task and evening scenes.
